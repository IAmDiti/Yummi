Privacy Policy

What are we eating tonight — that's the only question Yummi needs an answer to. This page explains exactly what the app collects along the way, why, and how to have any of it deleted.

Effective 16 September 2026 Accounts are optional

Yummi ("the app", "we", "us") helps you decide what to cook: photograph the inside of your fridge, get a meal suggestion, and cook it with step-by-step guidance — all without an account. Creating an account additionally lets you post a photo of what you made, rate other people's dishes, and save dietary preferences.

What the app processes, and why

01

Account information

If you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide. Passwords are handled entirely by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never visible to us in plain text.

02

Profile

Your display name and dietary preferences (e.g. vegetarian, gluten free) are stored against your account. Dietary preferences filter your recommendations and are never shown to other users; your display name is shown publicly next to anything you post or rate — unless you turn on Post anonymously in your profile, in which case "***" is shown instead of your name from then on.

03

Fridge-scan photos

When you scan your fridge, the photo is resized on your device and sent to our backend, which forwards it to our AI provider (Anthropic) to identify the food in it. It's used only for that one request — not stored by Yummi, not saved to any database.

04

Photos you post of a dish

If you share a photo of something you cooked, that photo is uploaded and stored, and is publicly visible to everyone using the app in the Discover feed, alongside your display name (or "***" — see above), the recipe name, and your caption. It stays until you delete the post or your account. Yummi does not collect or store your location.

05

Ratings

Star ratings (1–5) you give other people's posts are stored and linked to your account. Other users only ever see the aggregated average for a post, never who rated it.

06

Your ingredient list

Stored only on your device, never uploaded for safekeeping. The ingredient names (text only) are sent to our backend and AI provider when you ask for a recommendation or cooking help.

07

Voice input

Speech recognition runs on your device's own operating system — Yummi receives only the resulting text, which is sent onward for cooking questions. Audio itself is never recorded, uploaded, or stored.

08

Network address

Your IP address is briefly recorded solely to rate-limit abuse, and expires automatically within 24 hours. Never sold, shared for advertising, or linked to any identity.

Who processes your data

ProviderRoleMore information
Supabase Authentication, database, file storage, and the backend that proxies AI requests and enforces rate limits supabase.com/privacy
Anthropic AI processing of fridge photos and text to identify ingredients, generate recommendations, and answer cooking questions anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising, and we run no third-party analytics or ad tracking.

Data retention

  • Fridge-scan photos and cooking-assistant text: not retained after the request completes.
  • Rate-limiting records (IP address): deleted automatically within 24 hours.
  • Your ingredient list: stays on your device until you remove items or uninstall the app.
  • Account, profile, dish posts, posted photos, and ratings: kept until you delete them, or your account — see below.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account at any time, two ways:

A

In the app — tap the account icon → Delete account → confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, profile, dietary preferences, dish posts, posted photos, and ratings.

B

Without the app — use the web page below and sign in with your account's email and password to request deletion.

Delete my account  →

If you no longer have access to the app or can't sign in, email ditidon@gmail.com and we'll delete your account manually. Deletion is irreversible; we keep no copy of deleted account data beyond what the law may require, and we have no such requirement today.

Children

Yummi is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Use the core scan → recommend → cook flow without ever creating an account, and without granting camera or microphone access — add ingredients manually instead.
  • Creating an account, posting a dish, and rating posts are all optional, and you can post and rate under "***" instead of your name (Profile → Post anonymously).
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device, but not your account or anything already posted — use the deletion option above for that.
